How I Plan a Digital Marketing Campaign

Every project, big or small, follows the same simple steps.

  1. 🎯

    1. Set goals

    Decide what success means: visits, leads, calls or sales.

  2. 🔎

    2. Research

    Study the audience, competitors and the keywords people search.

  3. 🧭

    3. Choose channels

    Pick the right mix of SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads and social media.

  4. ✍️

    4. Create

    Write content, design ads and set up tracking.

  5. 📈

    5. Measure and improve

    Check traffic, clicks, cost per lead and conversions, then adjust.

Digital Marketing Project FAQs

What is a digital marketing project?

A digital marketing project is a planned effort to reach a business goal online, such as more website visitors, leads or sales, using channels like SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads and social media.

What steps are in a digital marketing campaign?

Typical steps are setting goals, researching the audience and keywords, choosing channels, creating content or ads, tracking results and improving over time.

How do you measure digital marketing results?

Common measures include website traffic, search rankings, click-through rate, cost per lead, conversions and return on ad spend, tracked with Google Analytics and ad platform dashboards.

Are these projects real client work?

The projects on this page are sample concepts that show how I plan and think. They are not real client results.
