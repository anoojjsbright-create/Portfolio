Frequently Asked Questions

What does a digital marketing freelancer do?

A freelance digital marketer plans and runs online promotion for a business: SEO, Google Ads, Facebook and Instagram ads, social media content and reporting, without the cost of a full agency.

Do you offer digital marketing services in Nagercoil?

Yes. I am based in Nagercoil and help local businesses in Kanyakumari district, and I also work remotely with clients across Tamil Nadu and India.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO earns free, long-term visibility in Google search results. Google Ads is paid advertising that can bring visitors quickly while the budget runs. Many businesses use both.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is gradual. Many sites see early movement in a few months, but timing depends on competition, website quality and content. Anyone promising instant first-page rankings should be treated with caution.

Is Facebook and Instagram advertising good for small businesses?

Often yes. Meta Ads let a small business target people by location, age and interests, so even a modest budget can reach nearby customers.

How can AI tools like ChatGPT help my marketing?

AI tools speed up keyword research, ad copy ideas, content outlines and reporting. I use them as an assistant, with human review for accuracy and brand voice.
