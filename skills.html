What These Skills Mean in Practice

Plain-language explanations for business owners.

SEO

Improving your website so it appears higher on Google for the words customers search, through better content, titles, links and site speed.

Google Ads

Paid ads that show at the top of Google when people search for your product or service. You pay when someone clicks.

Meta Ads

Paid ads on Facebook and Instagram, shown to people chosen by location, age and interests.

Social media marketing

Posting useful, consistent content and talking with followers to build trust in your brand.

Content marketing

Blogs, captions and videos that answer customer questions and bring people to your business.

Google Analytics

A free tool that shows who visits your website, where they come from and what they do there.

Local SEO

Helping nearby customers find you on Google Maps and in “near me” searches.

AI tools

Using assistants such as ChatGPT to speed up research and drafts, with human review for accuracy.

Digital Marketing Skills FAQs

What skills does a digital marketer need?

Core skills include SEO, paid advertising (Google Ads and Meta Ads), social media marketing, content creation, email marketing, analytics and clear communication.

Is SEO a good skill to learn in 2026?

Yes. People still search on Google every day, and businesses need help being found. SEO also works well alongside content marketing and paid ads.

Can I learn digital marketing without a marketing degree?

Yes. Digital marketing is learned through practice, free courses, certifications and real projects. I come from a biotechnology background and am building these skills step by step.

Which tools are used for digital marketing?

Common tools include Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Canva and AI assistants like ChatGPT.
